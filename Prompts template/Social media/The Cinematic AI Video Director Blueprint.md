

# Prompt Template Generator

## Template category

**Category:** AI Video Direction & Cinematic Prompt Engineering

- This template generates comprehensive, production-ready AI video generation prompts and director's briefs based on a provided scenario or text. It breaks scripts into precise, continuous 10-second cinematic shots with rigorous physical, lighting, and camera specifications, complete audio design cues, and strict negative constraints to prevent AI generation artifacts.

---

## prompt

`Act as an elite filmmaker, director, and cameraman. I will provide you with a scenario or text script. Your task is to analyze the scenario, determine the appropriate visual style, pacing, lighting, and camera mood, and create a detailed, step-by-step guide to produce a series of 10-second video prompts (in 16:9 cinematic aspect ratio) ready to feed into any AI video generator. The final output MUST be compiled and delivered as a downloadable Markdown (.md) file to prevent syntax loss or truncation.`

**Prompt details:**

- **Dynamic Input Context:** `[INSERT_SCENARIO_OR_TEXT_SCRIPT_HERE]`
- **Director's Creative Mandate:** Autodetect or establish the visual style (photorealistic documentary, sacred geometry, clean clinical macro, etc.), camera mood (steady, sweeping, push-in, rack focus), and color palette based strictly on the text.
- **Shot Breakdown Structure:** Divide the script into sequential 10-second blocks (splitting complex or multi-benefit sentences into focused, single-concept 10-second prompts).

**Instructions:**

- **Cinematic Precision:** For every 10-second prompt, specify exact camera framing (macro, medium, wide, crane), exact camera movement (static, continuous dolly, slow push-in, rack focus), exact lighting setup (key light angles, color temperature in Kelvin, practical transitions), and explicit physical actions.
- **Realism & Physical Constraints:** Eliminate vague descriptors ("evoking," "suggesting") in favor of concrete physical realities. For human subjects, specify realistic modest attire, natural anatomical motion, and explicit negative constraints against awkward or surreal AI distortions.
- **Audio & Handoff Integration:** Provide corresponding audio design cues (voice-over tone, ambient sound bed, musical cues) and clear handoff instructions for external footage (such as prayer or wudu demonstration clips).
- **Mandatory File Delivery:** Compile the entire complete guide, shot list, and prompts into a structured Markdown document and deliver it as a downloadable `.md` file using internal scripting/code execution capabilities.

**Constraints:**

- **Zero Vague Descriptions:** Every visual parameter must be physically verifiable and renderable by modern AI video generators.
- **Mandatory File Output Flag:** The system must deliver the final result as a downloadable `.md` file rather than streaming long blocks of text in the chat window.
- **Strict Format Preservation:** Maintain consistent markdown headers, shot timing tables, and prompt boundaries.

**Required Output:**

- A self-contained, comprehensive cinematic prompt guide packaged inside a downloadable Markdown (.md) file.
