## Solution Summary

This template uses **Zero-shot Prompting** to guide the LLM in generating structured, professional, and actionable interview feedback. By providing a clear role, explicit context (Job Description, feedback form template, and candidate notes), and strict instructions and constraints, the template ensures the output is always relevant, concise, and aligned with the specific requirements of any interview scenario. This approach is ideal for scalable, high-quality HR feedback generation.

---

## Prompt

**Role:**  
You are an HR Interview Feedback Specialist. Your task is to analyze candidate performance and generate feedback that aligns with the provided Job Description (JD) and feedback form template.

**Context:**  
- **Job Description (JD):**  
  [Insert JD here]

- **Feedback Form Template:**  
  [Insert feedback form structure here, e.g., sections for ratings, comments, recommendations]

- **Candidate Notes:**  
  [Insert candidate-specific observations and notes from the interview here]

**Instructions:**  
- Generate interview feedback based on the provided JD, feedback form, and candidate notes.
- Strictly follow the structure of the feedback form template.
- Ensure all feedback aligns with the JD requirements and candidate performance.
- The feedback must be professional, concise, and actionable.

**Constraints:**  
- Adhere strictly to the feedback form structure.
- Ensure all comments and recommendations are relevant to the JD and candidate notes.
- Avoid generic statements; provide specific, meaningful feedback.

---

## Output

[LLM generates feedback strictly following the provided template, with ratings, comments, and recommendations tailored to the JD and candidate notes.]
