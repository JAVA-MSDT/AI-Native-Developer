## Solution Summary

This template uses **Role-Based Prompting** to guide an LLM in generating distinct, persona-driven responses on any topic. By assigning clear roles, specifying tone, depth, and structure, and setting constraints, the prompt ensures nuanced, relevant, and audience-tailored outputs. This approach is ideal for multi-perspective articles, balanced analyses, and stakeholder reports.

---

## Prompt

You are writing about [TOPIC]. Generate multiple distinct responses, each from a specific role and perspective. For each role, use the assigned tone, context, and structure. The target audience is [TARGET AUDIENCE]. The purpose is to provide a balanced, multi-perspective analysis.

**Roles and Instructions:**

1. **[Role 1: e.g., Subject Matter Expert]**  
   - Tone: [e.g., Professional, evidence-based]  
   - Length: [e.g., 150–200 words]  
   - Structure:  
     - [Section 1: e.g., Introduction to the topic]  
     - [Section 2: e.g., Key challenges or insights]  
     - [Section 3: e.g., Recommendations or solutions]  
   - Constraints: [e.g., Cite relevant research, focus on well-being]

2. **[Role 2: e.g., Organizational Leader]**  
   - Tone: [e.g., Strategic, pragmatic]  
   - Length: [e.g., 120–170 words]  
   - Structure:  
     - [Section 1: e.g., Overview of impact]  
     - [Section 2: e.g., Benefits and challenges]  
     - [Section 3: e.g., Initiatives or actions]  
   - Constraints: [e.g., Address both positives and negatives]

3. **[Role 3: e.g., Individual Experience]**  
   - Tone: [e.g., Relatable, candid]  
   - Length: [e.g., 100–150 words]  
   - Structure:  
     - [Section 1: e.g., Personal account]  
     - [Section 2: e.g., Pros and cons]  
     - [Section 3: e.g., Suggestions or reflections]  
   - Constraints: [e.g., Use first-person, share real-life feelings]

[Add more roles as needed.]
