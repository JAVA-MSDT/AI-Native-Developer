## Solution Summary

This template applies the **Role-Based Prompt Framework** by assigning the LLM the role of a Career Advisor. It ensures the model delivers structured, empathetic, and context-aware career guidance tailored to an individual’s interests, skills, and constraints. The explicit output format and constraint directives prevent generic advice and ensure actionable, relevant recommendations. This template is ideal for career counseling, student advising, or workforce development scenarios.

---

## Prompt

Assume the role of a Career Advisor. Based on the following student profile, provide three tailored career path recommendations. For each, use the formatting template below and ensure salary ranges and work-life balance are stated consistently. Present your advice in a friendly, encouraging tone.

**Explicit Constraints:**  
- Ensure all recommendations align with the student’s listed skills, interests, and constraints.
- Avoid overly generic advice; favor feasible paths for a [beginner/intermediate/advanced] in [relevant skill area].
- Recommendations must be practical for someone seeking [hybrid/remote/on-site] options and [part-time/full-time] study.

**Student Profile:**  
- **Interests:** [e.g., Creative arts, technology, communication]  
- **Skills:** [e.g., Strong writing, visual design, basic coding (Python), public speaking]  
- **Constraints:** [e.g., Prefers hybrid/remote work, interested in part-time study, located in a major city with access to universities and tech companies]

---

## Output Formatting Template

For each career path, use the following structure:

- **Career Path Name**
  - **Description:** [Brief overview of the career]
  - **Salary Range & Work-Life Balance:** [Consistent format, e.g., “Entry-level: $X–$Y/year; Typical work-life balance: …”]
  - **Skill-Building Recommendations / Next Steps:** [Actionable steps, certifications, internships, etc.]
  - **Pros:** [Bullet points]
  - **Cons:** [Bullet points]
