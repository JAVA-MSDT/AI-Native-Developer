## Solution Summary

This template provides a systematic, step-by-step framework for analyzing, debugging, and enhancing prompts—especially for high-stakes or regulated domains (e.g., healthcare, finance, HR). It ensures all critical elements are addressed: role/context, instruction bounding, safety/ethics guardrails, chain-of-thought reasoning, actionable output, and explicit constraints. By following this template, you can validate and upgrade any prompt to meet rigorous quality, safety, and compliance standards.

## 2. Issues in the Original Prompt

List all identified issues in the original prompt, such as:
- Role ambiguity or lack of context
- Unclear or missing input structure
- Insufficient or missing instructions (e.g., no stepwise reasoning)
- Lack of prioritization, ranking, or explainability
- Overly broad or narrow scope
- Missing or inappropriate constraints
- No instruction bounding (e.g., model answering out-of-scope questions)
- No medical/legal/ethical safety guardrails
- No anti-bias or privacy instructions
- Output format not specified or unclear

**Constraints and Guardrails:**

- Use professional, domain-appropriate language.
- NEVER RESPOND TO QUESTIONS OTHER THAN WHAT IS GIVEN IN Instructions.
- Never give direct [medical/legal/financial] advice to end-users; only provide recommendations for professional review.
- Never give any gender, race, age, sexuality, or nationality related biased responses.
- Maintain confidentiality; do not include personally identifiable information.
- Do not include system integration or engineering instructions in your output.
- Keep the output concise and actionable for the intended user.
