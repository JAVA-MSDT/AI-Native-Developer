## Solution Summary

This template leverages the **R.O.S.E.S. (Recognize, Organize, Suggest, Evaluate, Solve) Prompting Framework** to guide an LLM through a comprehensive, stepwise approach to system design, architecture, and solution analysis. By explicitly structuring the prompt around business context, modular architecture, AI/technique suggestions, trade-off evaluation, and actionable solutions, it ensures thorough, stakeholder-ready outputs for any complex engineering or product scenario.

---

## Prompt

You are a senior [domain/technology] architect. Using the R.O.S.E.S. (Recognize, Organize, Suggest, Evaluate, Solve) framework, design a scalable, modular, and intelligent solution for the following scenario:

**Scenario:**  
[Describe the business or technical challenge here.]

Follow these steps:

1. **Recognize:**  
   - Summarize the business goals and key requirements for the system or solution.
2. **Organize:**  
   - Identify and describe the core architecture modules (e.g., data ingestion, processing, model inference, API delivery).
   - Map out the interactions and data flows between these components.
3. **Suggest:**  
   - Propose AI/ML-driven or advanced technical enhancements (e.g., algorithms, hybrid models, contextual features).
   - Recommend deployment strategies, retraining/data refresh pipelines, or other relevant operational practices.
4. **Evaluate:**  
   - Analyze trade-offs in technique/algorithm selection, latency vs. accuracy, batch vs. real-time, etc.
   - Address scalability, observability, and fault-tolerance.
5. **Solve:**  
   - Present a modular system architecture (textual or diagrammatic).
   - Identify potential bottlenecks and propose resolutions.
   - Summarize how your design meets the business goals and technical requirements.

Your response should be structured, actionable, and suitable for presentation to both technical and business stakeholders.
